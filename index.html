Built-In Safeguards

Why this version is harder to game

⚖️

Risk-adjusted ranking

ROI is divided by drawdown, so a lucky last-minute gamble doesn't outrank steady, disciplined trading. See the formula.

📅

Spread-out volume rule

Lots must be traded across at least 3 separate weeks, closing the "5 lots in one afternoon" loophole.

🔒

Server-verified entries

Leaderboard scores and raffle entries are never self-submitted — they're granted automatically from verified account activity.

Prizes are structured across different categories:

  • Grand Raffle: Conducted in collaboration with the Tickmill challenge organizers during an exclusive event held in a private venue to celebrate alongside our clients and winners.
  • Quarterly Prizes: Awarded directly to the trader who achieves the highest ROI (Return on Investment) for the respective quarter.

  • Winner Notification & Claiming: Organizers will directly contact winners via email or phone call with full instructions on how to claim their reward.
